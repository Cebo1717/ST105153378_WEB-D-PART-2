🙂Welcome to 360WAVING🌊

Welcome to 360WAVING. We help you get 360 waves all day. We sell durags, wave brushes and pomades.

Founded in 2024 in Durban, KwaZulu-Natal. We started as an Instagram page selling durags and brushes because wave products in SA were imported, overpriced and often fake. Our goal is to make elite wave culture accessible to everyone - from first ripple to 720 waves.

What we sell📦

  • Durags - Silky and Velvet for compression
  • Wave Caps - Double-layer stocking caps
  • Wave Brushes - Hard for wolfing, Medium for training, Soft for finishing
  • Pomades and Butters - For hold and shine
  • Oils and Moisturizers - To keep hair hydrated
  • Shampoos and Conditioners - Wave-friendly wash
  • Complete Wave Kits - Beginner to Elite (Durag + Cap + Brushes + Pomade + Oil)

Why Choose 360WAVING?

We are Durban's home of wave culture. We sell only authentic, premium products. We provide education and community, not just products. Nationwide delivery across South Africa.
