About Us

Who We Are: 360WAVING is a Beauty, Grooming & Streetwear brand based in Durban, KwaZulu-Natal. We are an e-commerce brand with nationwide delivery. We started in 2024 as an Instagram page selling durags and wave brushes.

We love waves. We want everyone to have 360 waves. We saw a problem - wavers in SA were paying too much for imported, overpriced and often fake products. Our mission is to make elite wave culture accessible, affordable and authentic. See what we stock on the products page.

What drives us

Our Mission

To empower every waver with premium, authentic products, education, and community to achieve their best waves - from first ripple to 720.

Our Vision

To be South Africa's most trusted and celebrated wave culture destination - the home of waves, drip, and discipline.

Our Values

  • Authentic - No fake products
  • Premium - Only best quality
  • Community - Education and tutorials
  • Discipline - Waves = dedication

Target Audience

  • Beginners 15-30 starting their wave journey
  • Dedicated wavers - 360, 540, 720 patterns
  • Barbers and salons - ask about bulk orders
  • Streetwear enthusiasts - drip culture
