Welcome to 360WAVING 🌊 - 360 Waves All Day

We help you get 360 waves all day. We sell durags, wave brushes and pomades - authentic wave-care products with nationwide delivery in South Africa.

Founded in 2024 in Durban, KwaZulu-Natal. We started as an Instagram page selling durags and brushes because wave products in SA were imported, overpriced and often fake. Our goal is to make elite wave culture accessible to everyone - from first ripple to 720 waves. Read our full story.
