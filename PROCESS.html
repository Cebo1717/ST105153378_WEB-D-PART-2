Process On How To Get 360 Waves - Step By Step

From first ripple to 720 - instruction guide by 360WAVING Durban

What You Need

  • Hard Brush - For wolfing
  • Medium Brush - For daily training
  • Soft Brush - For finishing and shine
  • Durag - Silky Velvet
  • Wave Cap - Compression
  • Pomade / Oil - Moisturizer

Step 1: Brush - The Foundation

Brush from crown outward. 360 = brush all directions. 30 minutes minimum per day. Hard brush when wolfing, Medium daily, Soft for polishing.

Step 2: Moisturize

Apply oil and pomade to keep hair hydrated. No dry scalp. Use 360WAVING oils and butters.

Step 3: Wash

Wash once a week with wave shampoo. Brush in shower with conditioner. Keep pattern.

Step 4: Compress - Durag On

After brushing, put on durag + wave cap. Sleep with durag every night. Double compression = deeper waves.

Step 5: Wolfing

Don't cut for 4-6 weeks. Keep brushing and duragging. Then get low cut. Repeat. Waves get deeper each wolf.

Step 6: Repeat - Discipline

Waves = Discipline. Brush, moisturize, durag, repeat. 540 and 720 comes with time.

Drip = Culture. Discipline = Waves. 360 Waves All Day.

Final Result - 360 Waves All Day

This is what you get when you follow the process:

Perfect 360 waves + clean beard fade = discipline + drip.
